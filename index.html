Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers
before you book.

How does KaamSaathi match me with a partner?+

Our matching engine scores partners based on requested skills, live proximity to your address, availability, customer rating history, and response speed.

How does KYC and identity verification work?+

Partners submit masked government identification and address documents. Our admin verification desk audits the credentials before activating the profile. We strictly never store or display raw Aadhaar numbers.

Can I schedule a service for later or book ASAP?+

Yes! During checkout, select either ASAP (arriving in 15–25 minutes) or pick a scheduled date and time slot that suits your schedule.

What payment methods are supported?+

You can pay via UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards, or pay cash directly after service completion.
